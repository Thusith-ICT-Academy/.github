## 🎓 About Us

**Thusith ICT Academy** is a modern ICT education platform focused on building the next generation of technology leaders in Sri Lanka.

We provide practical and engaging ICT education for school students while preparing them for the rapidly evolving digital world.

Our academy is guided by a professional Software Engineer with over **10 years of industry experience**.
